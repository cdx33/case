About

I’m Aron, based in London. I’m interested in understanding how people work, testing useful ideas with AI, and helping teams turn those ideas into practical changes.

Current work

Since June 2026, I’ve worked at Venn Apps as a Junior Implementation Specialist. I configure Shopify mobile apps through JSON files and CMS content to match supplied designs. I also set up metafields and metaobjects, discuss setup requirements with clients by email and calls, and coordinate requests that need engineering support.

Product and research experience

At Chapter 2, I worked from existing recruiter interview transcripts to create an eight-stage workflow map adopted by the product team. I also mapped the candidate booking journey and developed in-product messaging. My MSc in AI in User Experience Engineering at City St George’s, University of London included building and evaluating an AI research prototype. Before that, I earned a First Class BSc in Media Production at De Montfort University.

How I use AI

I use Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini and Cursor to explore ideas and build prototypes with AI-generated code. My contribution is defining the problem, shaping workflows, iterating prompts and evaluating the result.

Alongside my day job, I’m developing Groundwork to explore how AI might help examine workplace processes. I’m looking for a junior product, AI implementation or product operations role where I can keep learning through practical work.
